Scope Creep Prevention & Quick ReferencePage 12 · Keep this handy during the project
Tips to Avoid Scope Creep
1.Never approve verbal change orders. Always get it in writing and log it in the Change Order Log FIRST — before any work begins on the change.
2.Check the Running Total before saying yes. If approved change orders are approaching 10% of your original budget, pause and reassess your priorities.
3.Build 15% contingency in from the start. That's already calculated on the Dashboard. Don't spend it unless you must — it's your emergency fund.
4.Review the Dashboard weekly. A small overage today becomes a large problem next month. Catching it early gives you options.
5.Match Room/Area names exactly across all tabs. Spelling and capitalization must be identical — "Kitchen" not "kitchen" or "KITCHEN".
6.Log every change order, no matter how small. A $150 change and a $200 change and a $300 change become $650 of scope creep you never approved in aggregate.
Weekly Review Checklist
Check Dashboard: is Total Spent within Revised Total Budget?
Check Change Order Log: any new changes requested this week? Log them before work starts.
Check Running Total on Change Order Log: approaching 10% threshold?
Check Payment Schedule: any overdue payments (red rows)?
Check Materials Tracker: any delayed deliveries? Any cost overages in the Variance column?
Update Room Status on Dashboard: are any rooms moving from "Not Started" to "In Progress"?
Review Contingency Remaining: is it eroding faster than expected?
Formula Quick Reference
Revised Total:
=Original Bid + Approved COs
Outstanding Balance:
=Amount Due − Amount Paid
Materials Variance:
=Actual Cost − Budgeted Cost
Contingency Budget:
=Original Budget × 0.15
10% CO Threshold:
=Original Budget × 0.10
