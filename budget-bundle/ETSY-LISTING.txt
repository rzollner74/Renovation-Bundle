📊 6-Tab Spreadsheet (CSV Pack for Google Sheets / Excel):

• Dashboard — One-screen summary: total budget, total spent, approved
  change orders, revised total, overall % complete, and 15% contingency
  tracker. Status auto-updates for every room.

• Scope & Budget by Room — Original bids for 11 rooms/trades (Kitchen,
  Primary Bath, Guest Bath, Living Room, Master Bedroom, Flooring,
  Exterior, Electrical, Plumbing, HVAC, Other). Revised totals and
  Amount Paid pull automatically from other tabs.

• Change Order Log — Log every change order with date, room, description,
  reason, cost impact, and approval status. Running Total auto-calculates.
  RED WARNING fires automatically if change orders exceed 10% of your
  original budget.

• Payment Schedule — Track every milestone payment by contractor, room,
  and scheduled date. Outstanding Balance auto-calculates. Overdue
  payments highlight automatically.

• Materials Tracker — Log every material order: item, supplier, budgeted
  vs. actual cost, order date, lead time, delivery date, and status.
  Variance column flags overages in red instantly.

• Instructions — Plain-English setup guide with step-by-step instructions,
  scope creep prevention tips, color coding guide, and troubleshooting.

• Professional printable workbook — open in Chrome, click Print
• Project overview, room budget sheets, change order log forms,
  payment schedule, materials tracker, and scope creep reference card

❓ FAQ
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Q: Does this work with Microsoft Excel?
A: Yes. Open each CSV with File → Open. Formulas are included as
   paste-ready text in each tab's FORMULA REFERENCE section.

Q: What if I have more rooms than the 11 pre-loaded?
A: Add rows in the Scope & Budget tab, then copy the formula from the
   row above into the new row. Update the Dashboard room list to match.

Q: Does it work for smaller projects (just one room)?
A: Yes — just use the rows you need and leave the rest blank.

Q: How do I add more change orders than the pre-loaded rows?
A: Insert a new row, right-click the row above, Copy, then right-click
   the new row, Paste. The formulas will copy automatically.

Q: Is the contingency percentage customizable?
A: Yes — the formula reference section shows the contingency formula.
   Change 0.15 to any percentage you prefer (e.g., 0.10 for 10%).

Q: Is this a physical item?
A: No — instant digital download delivered immediately after purchase.
