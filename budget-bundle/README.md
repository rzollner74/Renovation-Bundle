## Quick Start

### Option A — Google Sheets / Excel
1. Open `csv/00_README_Import_Instructions.txt` for step-by-step setup.
2. Import each numbered CSV as a separate tab.
3. Paste the formulas from each FORMULA REFERENCE section.
4. Enter your Total Budget in the yellow Dashboard cell first — everything else auto-calculates.

### Option B — Printable PDF Workbook
1. Open `printable/Home-Renovation-Budget-Tracker-Printable.html` in Chrome or Safari.
2. Click **"Print / Save as PDF →"** (top-right button).
3. Paper: Letter | Background graphics: ON | Save as PDF.

---

## How the Spreadsheet Works

1. **Enter your Total Budget** on the Dashboard (yellow cell B6). All other totals calculate automatically.
2. **Enter Original Bids** in the Scope & Budget by Room tab (yellow column C).
3. **Log every change order** in the Change Order Log — the Running Total and 10% warning update automatically.
4. **Log payments** in the Payment Schedule — Amount Paid flows back to the Dashboard automatically.
5. **Track materials** in the Materials Tracker — Variance highlights overages in red.

> **Critical:** Room/Area names must match exactly across all tabs (spelling and capitalization).
> Never delete a formula cell (any cell that starts with =).

## Status Color Key

| Color | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| Green | On Budget — spending is at or below revised total |
| Yellow | Watch — spending is between 90–100% of revised total |
| Red | Over Budget — spending has exceeded the revised total |
| Red row (Payment Schedule) | Overdue payment — scheduled date passed, no payment recorded |
