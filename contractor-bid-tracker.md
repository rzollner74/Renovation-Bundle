# Contractor Comparison & Bid Tracker

Google Sheets spreadsheet created via `gws` CLI.

**URL:** https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1Ct-_h_sDU-qgn897WheXkrsaGjqEYWafN2QKegkBIiI/edit

## Tabs

1. **Dashboard** — Side-by-side contractor summary with auto-flagged lowest bid (sage green), top score (terracotta), and recommended hire decision cell
2. **Bid Comparison** — 10 contractor input rows with expiry highlighting and Materials + Labor Gap auto-flag
3. **Contractor Scoring** — Auto price scoring, manual interview scores, weighted score (Price 30% / Timeline 20% / Comms 20% / Refs 20% / Gut 10%), auto-rank
4. **License & Insurance Verification** — Expiry-highlighted verification tracker with fully-verified row highlighting
5. **Reference Check Log** — 30 pre-loaded rows (3 per contractor), quality rating conditional formatting, per-contractor average summary
6. **Contract Checklist** — 10-item pre-signing checklist with completion tracker and status indicator
7. **Instructions** — Plain English homeowner guide for using the tracker in order

## Design

Matches Home Renovation Budget Tracker theme: `#2C3E50` headers, `#E07A5F` terracotta alerts, `#7D9B76` sage green verified/on-track, `#FEF9F0` cream input cells.
