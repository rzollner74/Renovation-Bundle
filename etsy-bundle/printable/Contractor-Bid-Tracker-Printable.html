Key Questions to Ask Every Contractor
1.Have you done projects like this before? Can I see photos or visit a comparable completed job?
2.Will you personally be on-site every day, or will a crew supervisor manage day-to-day work?
3.What subcontractors will you use, and are they licensed and insured?
4.What is your process if we discover something unexpected mid-project (rotted wood, bad wiring, etc.)?
5.How do you handle change orders, and what are your payment terms and schedule?

Minimum Verification Steps
License: Verify active at your state licensing board website.
General Liability: Request COI sent directly from insurer to you.
Workers Comp: Required if contractor has employees. Verify with carrier.
Bonding: Recommended for projects over $5,000.
BBB / Reviews: Check BBB, Google Reviews, and court records.
